# Product guide

## Hosted clickngoal

The hosted service at https://goal.clickn.dev/ brings goals and social progress into one place. A profile can contain personal or shared goals. A progress entry can record a result, while a saved calendar mark identifies a completed day. Selecting a date is navigation, not completion. A note and a completion mark have distinct meanings.

Goal cards show saved completed days, current streak, best streak and today's status. Public progress can appear in the feed. Communities, profiles, comments and reactions support discussion. Visibility controls decide what other people can see. Your feed setting can hide your own reports without deleting them.

Settings are organised by profile, appearance, privacy, feed, notifications and account access. Russian and English are available. Themes follow the selected light/dark/device mode. Email/password and Telegram are sign-in options. Achievements and reminders depend on your account settings and browser/native support. Some additional capabilities are Premium features; this repository does not promise that all hosted features are free.

## Public foundation demo

The community demo implements a small local example: create a personal/public demo goal, select a calendar day, mark completion, write/update a daily note, inspect a fictional feed, react and comment. Separate controls make completion and notes explicit. It demonstrates the extracted calendar and theme modules.

All changes are stored in your browser. Fictional authors do not represent registered accounts. Privacy checks are examples of local domain rules, not a secure server boundary. There is no sign-in, server synchronisation, push delivery, file upload, payment processing or connection to production. The source is a foundation for development, not full multi-user self-hosting.
